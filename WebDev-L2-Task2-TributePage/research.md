# Ahmed Zewail — Research Notes

## Verified facts used in the page

- Ahmed Hassan Zewail was born on 26 February 1946 in Damanhur, Egypt, and grew up in Desouk.
- He studied chemistry at Alexandria University, then completed his PhD at the University of Pennsylvania under Robin M. Hochstrasser.
- He became known for pioneering femtochemistry, the study of chemical reactions on femtosecond timescales.
- He received the 1999 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for studies of the transition states of chemical reactions using femtosecond spectroscopy.
- His work used ultrashort laser flashes to observe molecular changes during chemical reactions.
- He also contributed to ultrafast electron diffraction and scientific education.
- He died on 2 August 2016 in Pasadena, California.
